# Kairn logo concept prompts

These prompts were used with the built-in image generator. Each produces one standalone concept on an opaque background.

## Shared Cairn

Use case: logo-brand. Design one polished logo concept for Kairn, a shared journey journal for friends. Square flat warm paper canvas #FBFAF6, solid charcoal ink #20221D. The design is a centered vertical lockup: one distinctive standalone emblem in the upper middle, then the exact word “Kairn” underneath, capital K and lowercase airn. Confident humanist grotesque lettering in the spirit of Bricolage Grotesque, optical kerning. Emblem about 38% of canvas width, word about the same width, generous outer margins. Crisp single-color vector-like edges, suitable for stamping onto the inside cover of a field notebook. Plain field, no grain, shadow, gradients, scenery, mockup, extra type, slogans or framing. Compose a low, off-balance trail cairn of exactly three chunky, irregular, horizontally laid stone silhouettes. The capstone leans left, the middle stone projects right, and the long foot stone anchors left. Chisel one distinctive almost level face on each piece. Two slender open channels of paper follow the joins across the stack like segments of a switchback trail. Hand-cut, quietly witty, compact and instantly drawable from memory. Avoid centered spa-stone ovals, a mountain outline, or a drawn winding road.
